Vaska

Go through a memory card one photo at a time, keep the best with a single key, and import only your keepers to Photos.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store.

One photo at a time

Keep with P, reject with X and rate with 1–5. Zoom to 100% to check focus.

Everything at a glance

Kept, rejected, rated and already imported photos are clearly marked.

Only the keepers

Import into a new or existing album in Photos. RAW+JPEG pairs stay together.

The card stays untouched

Vaska never changes or deletes anything on the card, and collects no data.

“Vaska” is Swedish for panning for gold: the gravel is washed away and the nuggets are left. That’s what Vaska does with your photos.
