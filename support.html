Vaska Support

Cull the photos on a memory card and import only the keepers to Photos.

Getting started

Insert a memory card, or choose File > Open Folder. The first time you open a card, Vaska asks for permission to read it: click Allow in the panel that opens on the card. Vaska remembers the card after that.

Press P to keep a photo, X to reject it and 1–5 to rate it. The arrow keys move between photos. When you’re done, click Import in the toolbar.

What happens to rejected photos?

Nothing. Only the photos you keep are imported. Rejected photos stay on the card, because Vaska never changes or deletes anything on it. Erase the card in your camera when you’re ready.

How do I import everything?

Choose Image > Keep All Unmarked, then click Import. Photos you have rejected are left out.

Can I see photos I have already imported?

Yes. Choose All Photos or Imported in the sidebar. Imported photos are marked, and Vaska won’t import them again.

Can Vaska open when I insert a card?

Yes. Turn on “Open Vaska when a memory card is inserted” in the window that’s shown when no card is open, or in Vaska > Settings. You can turn it off the same way.

Keyboard shortcuts

Choose Help > Keyboard Shortcuts in Vaska for the full list.
