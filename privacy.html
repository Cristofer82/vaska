Privacy Policy

Vaska for Mac · Last updated September 28, 2026

Vaska doesn’t collect, store or share any personal data. Everything Vaska does happens on your Mac.

Your photos

Vaska reads the photos on the memory cards and folders you open, to show them to you. It never changes, moves or deletes anything on the card.

The photos you choose to import are added to your Photos library on your Mac. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs them as usual under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Access to Photos

Vaska asks for access to Photos the first time you import. It uses that access only to add the photos you keep, to list your albums, and to recognize photos that are already in your library, so that they aren’t imported twice.

What Vaska stores

Your marks and ratings, the list of photos you have imported and your settings are saved in Vaska’s own folder on your Mac. They never leave your Mac, and you can remove them by deleting the app.

No tracking

Vaska has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn’t connect to the internet.

Questions

If you have questions about privacy in Vaska, see the support page.
